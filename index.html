Good questions, clear answers

Before you begin.

Here are a few things people often want to know.

Can Innovotix help automate a whole home?+

Yes. We can help bring lighting, climate, curtains, access and other connected devices together. The right plan depends on your home and what you want to control.

Can we start with just one room?+

Absolutely. Smart-home projects can begin with the spaces and systems that matter most to you, then grow over time.

What does a school robotics lab include?+

A lab can be planned around your school’s needs and budget, with robotics kits, IoT and electronics activities, 3D printing equipment and support to get started.

Are the kits suitable for beginners?+

Innovotix offers different ways to explore, including approachable kits for young learners and project-based tools for deeper experimentation. We can help you choose what fits.

How do we get started?+

Tell us a little about your home, school or idea. We’ll arrange a conversation and recommend a useful next step.
